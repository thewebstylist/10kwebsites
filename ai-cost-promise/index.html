Part one

The Cost

Ai runs on electricity, water and silicon. As usage scales, the bill is rising, and much of it is paid far from the screen.

Electricity · data centers

Electricity

Electricity · 2024

415TWh

Global data-center electricity use in 2024, about 1.5% of the world’s total. It has grown around 12% a year for the past five years.

Growth · 2025

+17%

The jump in data-center electricity use in a single year, even as grid connections and equipment supply tightened.

Projection · 2030

~945TWh

The IEA’s Base Case for 2030: more than double today, just under 3% of global electricity, and slightly more than Japan uses in a year. Projection

Concentration · United States

4.4% → 12%

Share of US electricity used by data centers in 2023 (176 TWh), and the top of the projected range for 2028. Local grids feel this first.

Water · cooling and power

Water

Water · per year

~148bn gallons

Estimated global data-center water use each year. Most of it is not the cooling towers you can see, but the water consumed generating the electricity. Estimate

Projection · 2030

~320bn gallons

Where that figure could be by 2030. Researchers project Ai alone could withdraw 1.1 to 1.7 trillion gallons of water in 2027, more than four to six Denmarks use in a year. Projection

Hidden share · United States, 2023

17bn gal direct

US data centers used around 17 billion gallons of water cooling their servers, and an estimated 211 billion more indirectly through the power plants that supply them.

One company · 2025

+34%

Rise in Google’s water consumption in one year, to about 10.9 billion gallons, more than double its 2021 level, mostly from data centers. Company-reported

Carbon

Small share, steep slope.

Data centers produce under 1% of global CO₂ today. The concern is the trajectory: indirect emissions are projected to grow by almost 80% this decade in the IEA’s central case, and 2.5 times in its high-adoption “Lift-Off” case.

It shows up in corporate ledgers too. Microsoft’s total emissions in its 2025 report were 23.4% above its 2020 baseline, which it attributes to Ai and cloud growth. Company-reported

Per prompt

One question, very different answers.

Estimates of the energy behind a single text prompt have fallen roughly tenfold as measurement has improved. The best current figures sit close to a 2009 web search.

The catch is scale and type: billions of prompts add up, and long reasoning chains, images and video can use many times more than a short text answer.

Part two

The Promise

Where Ai earns its footprint: discoveries that would have taken decades, and help that reaches people who were waiting.

Medicine · structure to screening

Medicine

Protein structures

200M+

Structures predicted by AlphaFold, nearly every cataloged protein. Determining one experimentally can take years; the database is open to all, with DeepMind reporting over 3 million users in 190+ countries. Usage company-reported

Recognition

2024

The Nobel Prize in Chemistry went to Ai-driven protein structure prediction and computational protein design, a first for the field.

Drug discovery

18months

From a new target to a drug candidate for lung fibrosis, with both found using generative Ai. Phase IIa results in Nature Medicine (2025) showed it was safe and well tolerated; a Phase III trial began in 2026. Efficacy is not yet proven. Timeline company-reported

Earlier detection

+29% detection

In Sweden’s MASAI randomised trial, Ai-supported mammography found 29% more cancers with no rise in false positives, cut radiologists’ reading workload by 44%, and led to about 12% fewer cancers surfacing between screenings.

    Closing

    Finding the Balance

    The question is not whether to use Ai, but how to make each answer worth its footprint.

    For individuals

    Use a search engine for simple lookups

    Facts, opening hours and definitions rarely need a generative model.

    Ask once, ask well

    A clear, complete prompt beats five regenerations. Batch related questions together.

    Right-size the model

    Save deep-reasoning and video modes for tasks that need them; smaller models handle most everyday work.

    Switch off what you don’t use

    Disable automatic Ai summaries and suggestions you never read.

    For companies

    Measure and disclose per-query impact

    Publish energy, water and carbon per request with a clear method, as Google and Mistral began to in 2025.

    Chase efficiency relentlessly

    Google reports a 33× cut in energy per median prompt in one year through model, hardware and serving improvements.

    Run on clean power, at the right time and place

    Match workloads to low-carbon hours and grids, and add new clean supply rather than only buying certificates.

    Respect local water limits

    Choose low-water cooling in stressed regions and account for the larger, indirect water in electricity.

    Every answer has a cost. The best ones repay it.
