# Sterling Creations Ai: house style rules

These apply to every site, page, video and document made for the brand.

## "Ai", never "AI" (trademark)

- Always write the abbreviation for artificial intelligence as **Ai**: capital A, lowercase i.
- This includes the brand name: **Sterling Creations Ai**.
- It applies inside all-caps styles too. If a label is set in uppercase with CSS, wrap the
  word so it keeps its lowercase i (for example `<span class="ai-mark">Ai</span>` with
  `text-transform: none`).
- Exceptions: other companies' names and published titles keep their own spelling
  (OpenAI, Mistral AI, Epoch AI, Be My AI, a report titled "Energy and AI").

## Audience: United States

- Use US units: gallons, fluid ounces, tablespoons, miles, °F. No liters, milliliters or metric
  volumes in copy. Convert metric source figures and note the conversion in the citation.
- Energy stays in watt-hours / kWh / TWh (standard in the US). Emissions: "million metric tons".
- Use US spelling (center, program, honor, color, labeled) and US number formatting.

## Brand look (light theme)

- White / off-white grounds (#FFFFFF, #F7F8FC), charcoal text (#1A1A2E).
- Softened magenta (#F2A7E3, #E77FD0) and softened blue (#8FCBF5, #4DB3EE) for large areas;
  saturated #FF3BD4 and #0599F2 only for small accents. Montserrat throughout.
